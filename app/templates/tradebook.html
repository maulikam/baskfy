Import a Console tradebook

Kite Connect exposes only today's trades, so acquisition dates for anything bought earlier have to come from an export. Without them every tax review returns TAX_DATA_UNKNOWN and no sale is ever assumed tax-safe. This is a one-time seed: from then on the daily run records each session's fills itself.

  1. Open console.zerodha.com → Reports → Tradebook
  2. Pick a range that reaches back before your oldest open position, then download the CSV
  3. Upload it here. Re-importing the same file changes nothing.

Corporate actions

A tradebook records transactions. A bonus or a split changes what you hold with no transaction at all, so lots rebuilt from fills alone come out short and reconciliation reports a mismatch it cannot price. Record it here and the lots are re-derived.

The two are not interchangeable. A bonus share has nil cost and its holding period starts at allotment, so selling one is short-term tax on the full proceeds for a year. A split acquires nothing: cost per share divides and the original holding period is inherited, so a share bought two years ago stays long-term.

Ratio is new:old. A 1:1 bonus is 1 and 1. A share splitting into two is 2 and 1. Confirm the ex-date from Console rather than inferring it from a price gap: it decides when the holding period starts.
