This policy explains what personal data Baskfy collects, why, how long it is kept, and what you
can do about it. It is written against the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

The data fiduciary is RENIL, 703/2, Sector 4C, Gandhinagar, Gujarat 382006.

## 1. What we collect

**Because you gave it to us**

- Your email address, your name and your Google account identifier, received from Google when
  you sign in. Signing in with Google is the only way to use the Service, so this is required —
  the email address is your identity on the Service and where we send receipts and notices. We
  ask Google only for your name, your email address and the fact that Google has verified it. We
  never receive your Google password, and we never ask for one of our own.
- A different display name, if you set one. Optional.
- The contents of any message you send us through the support form.

**Because you used the Service**

- Your saved screens, column layouts, portfolios, backtests and preferences.
- Sign-in events and refresh-token records, which is what makes session revocation possible.
- Server logs containing your IP address, the request path and a request identifier.

**Because you paid us**

- Payment metadata from Razorpay: an order identifier, a payment identifier, the amount, and the
  status.
- The tax invoices we issued you.

**We never collect** your card or bank details — Razorpay handles the payment and we receive only
the outcome — and we never ask for a broker login, a trading credential or an API key for your
broker. The Service does not place trades and has no mechanism to.

## 2. Cookies

We set the following, and only the following:

| Cookie | Purpose | Refusable |
|---|---|---|
| Session token | Keeps you signed in | No — the Service cannot work without it |
| Refresh token | Renews your session without sending you back to Google | No |
| CSRF token pair | Prevents another site submitting forms as you | No |
| Consent record | Remembers this choice | No |
| Announcement dismissal | Stops a banner reappearing | No |

We run **no advertising network, no analytics tag and no third-party tracker**. The consent banner
defaults to essential-only and there is presently nothing else to consent to. If that ever changes,
the additional category will be off until you switch it on.

## 3. Why we are allowed to process it

For your account, your saved work and your session: because it is necessary to provide the service
you asked for.

For payment records and tax invoices: because Indian tax law requires us to keep them.

For security logs and rate-limit counters: because we have a legitimate need to keep the Service
and your account safe.

For anything else — and there is presently nothing else — because you consented, in which case you
may withdraw consent at any time.

## 4. Who we share it with

- **Google**, which authenticates you. When you sign in, Google tells us your name, your email
  address and your Google account identifier; Google separately knows that you signed in to this
  Service. Your relationship with Google is governed by Google's own privacy policy, not by this
  one.
- **Razorpay**, to take payment and issue receipts.
- **Amazon SES**, to deliver receipts and notices.
- **Amazon Web Services** and **Cloudflare R2**, which host the servers and the object storage the
  Service runs on.
- **Sentry**, which receives error reports. Our logging strips tokens and credentials before
  anything leaves the process.

We do not sell personal data and we do not share it for advertising.

**Where your data is processed.** Your account, your saved work, your payment records and the
servers that run the Service are in **India** — Amazon Web Services' Mumbai region
(`ap-south-1`), and Razorpay, which is an Indian company. Two things leave: **Google** processes
your sign-in in the **United States** and elsewhere, and **Sentry**, when error reporting is
enabled, receives redacted diagnostics in the **United States**. Object storage on **Cloudflare
R2** may be served from outside India.

**The mechanism.** Section 16 of the DPDP Act permits a Data Fiduciary to transfer personal data
for processing to any country outside India except one the Central Government has restricted by
notification. No such restriction applies to any of the transfers above. We do not rely on your
consent for these transfers, so you do not lose the Service by objecting to one — but if a country
we use is later restricted, we will stop transferring to it rather than ask you to agree.

## 5. How long we keep it

| Data | Kept for |
|---|---|
| Account, saved screens, portfolios | Until you delete the account |
| Session and refresh-token records | Until expiry or revocation |
| Payment records and tax invoices | Eight years, as Indian tax law requires |
| Consent records | For as long as the account exists, plus the statutory period |
| Server logs | 90 days |

## 6. Your rights

You can, from your profile page and without asking us:

- **See and export** everything we hold about you, as a machine-readable file.
- **Correct** your display name.
- **Delete** your account. Deletion is a seven-day soft delete you can reverse by signing in
  again; after that it is permanent.

One consequence is worth stating plainly: **if you have ever been issued a tax invoice, deleting
your account anonymises it rather than removing it.** The invoice and the payment record must
survive for the statutory retention period, and they are linked to an account row that therefore
cannot be dropped. Your email, name and saved work are erased; a stripped account row and the
invoices remain.

Changing your email address is not something the profile page can do. Your address is the one
Google gives us when you sign in, so it is changed in your Google account rather than here — and
because your account is identified by a stable Google account identifier rather than by the
address, changing it in Google signs you into the same Baskfy account, not a new one. If you need
the address on an already-issued invoice corrected, write to support.

## 7. Security

**We store no password.** Signing in is delegated to Google, so there is no credential of ours
to steal, guess or reset — the strength of your sign-in, and its recovery, are settings in your
Google account. The identity token Google issues is verified against Google's published keys on
every sign-in and is never written to a log. Refresh tokens are stored as SHA-256 digests rather
than in the clear. Sessions are httpOnly, SameSite cookies. All traffic is over TLS. Our logging
filter redacts secrets, and our error reporter receives redacted records.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your data we will notify you and the Data
Protection Board of India as the DPDP Act requires.
