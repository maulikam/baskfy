This policy sets out when a Decile subscription can be refunded. It is deliberately specific,
because a vague refund policy is a dispute waiting to happen.

## The short version

- **Monthly plan:** full refund within 7 days of a charge, if you have not exported data in that
  period.
- **Yearly plan:** full refund within 14 days of a charge, on the same condition.
- **Forever plan:** full refund within 14 days of purchase, on the same condition.
- **After those windows:** no refund, except where this policy or the law says otherwise.
- **Renewals:** cancel before the renewal date and you are not charged. A renewal charged after a
  cancellation you can evidence is always refunded in full.

## Why the export condition

The gated features are export, custom columns, historical ranks and backtests. Exporting a full
result set is the point at which the paid product has been *delivered* rather than merely made
available — the data is on your machine and cannot be returned. Reading the site, running screens
and looking at a factsheet do not count; downloading a CSV or a backtest artefact does.

If you exported and then found the product unsuitable, write to us anyway and say what happened.
The condition exists to stop a subscription being used as a one-off data purchase, not to catch
out someone who tried a feature once.

## When we refund outside those windows

- **We charged you twice for the same period.** Always refunded, at any time.
- **We charged you after you cancelled.** Always refunded.
- **The Service was materially unavailable for a substantial part of your billing period.** We
  refund pro rata for the affected period.
- **We materially reduced what your plan includes without notice.** Pro rata for the remainder of
  the paid period.

## When we do not refund

- Dissatisfaction with the *rankings themselves*, or with any investment outcome. Decile publishes
  arithmetic over market data and does not promise any result; a loss on a position is not a
  product defect. See the [Disclaimer](/disclaimer).
- A partly used period on a plan you simply stopped using. Cancel to stop the next renewal.
- Data gaps that are documented in the Service before you buy — the empty price-to-earnings column
  and the history start date are both stated on the FAQ. Read it first.
- An account terminated for a deliberate breach of the [Terms](/terms-conditions), in particular
  redistribution of data obtained from the Service.

## What "Forever" means for refunds

The Forever plan is a single payment for access for the lifetime of the Service, not your
lifetime. It is refundable within 14 days like the others, and after that it is not refundable at
all — including if the Service later closes. If we close the Service we will give as much notice as
we can and will publish what we intend to do about outstanding lifetime plans at that time; this
policy does not promise a refund in that case, and you should treat a Forever purchase as a
purchase rather than as a deposit.

## How to ask

Write through the [support page](/support) with the subject **Billing or invoices**, from the email
address on the account, and include the invoice number. There is no form to fill in and no
automated refund button.

## What happens next

We acknowledge within two working days. An approved refund is initiated with Razorpay within five
working days; the money reaches the original payment method on their timetable, typically five to
ten working days from that point, and we cannot make it faster.

Refunds are always to the original payment method. We do not refund to a different card, a
different account, or as credit.

The tax invoice for a refunded payment is credited, not deleted — a credit note is issued and both
documents stay in your account, because tax law requires the trail.
