## 2. What a ranking is, and what it is not

Baskfy computes stated formulas over end-of-day market data and sorts the results. A name at the
top of a screen has, on that date, scored higher on that formula than the names below it. That is
the entire content of the statement.

It is **not** a statement that the security is a good investment, that its price will rise, that
the ranking will persist, that the screen is a portfolio, or that the top of it is a sensible place
to put money. Historical momentum is a measurement of the past, and the past does not oblige the
future to resemble it.

## 3. Backtests

Any simulated performance shown in this Service is hypothetical. It was not achieved by anyone,
with real money, at those prices.

Our backtests are computed point-in-time — the engine cannot read data dated after the simulated
date — and every run states the transaction costs, slippage assumptions and rebalancing rule it
used. They still share every limitation that hypothetical results have: they include no tax, no
market impact beyond the modelled slippage, no liquidity constraint on the size you would actually
trade, and no account of the decisions a human would have made on the bad days.

**Past performance is not indicative of future results, and simulated past performance is not even
past performance.**

## 4. The data can be wrong

We take care and we publish our formulas so you can check them. We do not warrant that any value
is accurate, complete or current.

Specifically, and stated here rather than left to be discovered:

- The price-to-earnings column and its filter are empty for every instrument, because nothing in
  the pipeline fetches fundamentals.
- Published factor history begins on 1 November 2024. There is less history than a long-horizon
  study needs.
- Index membership before 2018 is reconstructed from published sources rather than recorded, and
  is labelled as reconstructed within the Service.
- Rights issues are not price-adjusted, because the adjustment needs a subscription price the
  exchange notice usually omits. Rather than guess, those series are left unadjusted and the
  action is reported.
- Backtest Sharpe and Sortino figures use a flat risk-free rate defaulting to zero, so they are
  excess-over-zero figures rather than what a performance report would call Sharpe.

If you find a figure that disagrees with the exchange, tell us through the
[support page](/support). We treat it as a defect.
