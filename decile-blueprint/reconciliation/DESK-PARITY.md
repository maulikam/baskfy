# Desk parity — M12, the gate the merge stands on

**Run 22 Aug 2026.** Data version 1; `ohlcv_daily` holding 1,145,922 bars, 2024-01-01 → 2026-08-21.
Both files were put through the desk's **unmodified** `app/scoring.py`; nothing in this exercise
touches the desk's code.

The question is not "do our factors match the export cell by cell" — `PARITY-FAMILIES.md` answers
that. It is **"does the merged engine pick the same stocks, in the same order?"**

## The corpus, and one file excluded

`kite-momentum-rebalancer/data/uploads/` holds two scans.

| file | date | rows | used |
|---|---|---:|---|
| `scan_1787143663_Investing_001__1_.csv` | 2026-08-18 | 271 | **yes — the oracle** |
| `sample_scan.csv` | 2026-08-14 | 56 | **no — a synthetic fixture** |

`sample_scan.csv` is generated by `tests/make_sample_scan.py`, whose own docstring says *"Replace
data/uploads/sample_scan.csv with a real weekly scan export before…"*. It contains invented rows
built to trip specific filter branches — `TINYCO` ("Micro Cap Ltd"), `CIRCUITX` ("Circuit Prone
Ltd"), `FALLENANG` ("Fallen Angel Ltd"), `DOWNTREND` ("Downtrend Ltd") — plus `SGBDE31III`, the
sovereign gold bond the desk must never touch. **Comparing a price engine against invented prices
measures nothing**, so it is excluded here and remains what it is: a unit-test fixture.

The 271-row file is the real weekly export the desk was scored, ordered and stopped against. It is
also, to the row and the column, the same export `docs/13` decodes and the parity test compares to
— which is the merge thesis restated as a fact about two directories.

## The one real delta, and its cause

`SHILPAMED` falls from **#10 to #57**, score 76.4 → 63.5. That is not reordering; it is a wrong
answer. Its column-by-column signature says exactly why:

| column | uploaded | generated |
|---|---:|---:|
| `ma_20` | 2096.75 | **2096.75** ✅ |
| `ma_50` | 2012.69 | **2012.69** ✅ |
| `ma_100` | 1878.66 | 2680.53 ❌ |
| `ma_200` | 1694.96 | 2853.18 ❌ |
| `absolute_return_one_year` | +55.51 | −21.31 ❌ |
| `away_from_high_one_year` | −1.58 | −41.24 ❌ |

**The short windows match to the paisa and the long ones are wildly high.** That is the signature
of an unapplied corporate action, and the series confirms it: SHILPAMED closes at **778.75 on
2025-10-01 and 384.95 on 2025-10-03** — a ratio of 0.4943, an unadjusted split. Windows that do not
reach back to October 2025 are perfect; every window that crosses it is wrong.

**It is not one stock.** Scanning all 271 for an unadjusted jump:

> **40 of 271 symbols — 14.8% — carry an unadjusted corporate action**, the earliest on 2024-01-05.
> Among them: `NESTLEIND` (×0.098), `BAJFINANCE` (×0.100), `ANGELONE` (×0.099), `PGEL` (×0.099),
> `SHRIRAMFIN` (×0.189), `V2RETAIL` (×4.30).

The cause is already documented: `corporate_action` holds **four rows** in total, because
`docs/DECISIONS.md` §21.8 records that NSE serves only a *recent* corporate-actions window. The
adjustment code is correct — M10 proved it reproduces CUPID's split and both bonuses exactly — it
simply has almost no actions to apply.

**So the dominant residual in both M11 and M12 is missing corporate-action history, not the window
rule.** That reframes the open question in `PARITY-FAMILIES.md`: a window disagreement of one bar
cannot flip a return from +55% to −21%, and an unadjusted 2:1 split can.

## Breadth (P1.9): not reconcilable as it stands

| | desk `breadth_readings` | pipeline `market_health_daily` |
|---|---|---|
| metric | `pct_above_20dma` | `pct_above_50dma`, `pct_above_200dma` |
| population | whatever scan CSV was uploaded | a named index universe |
| rows | 1, for 2026-08-14 | 12 universes for 2026-08-18 |

They measure **different things over different populations**, and there is no `pct_above_20dma` in
the pipeline at all. The desk's single row is also derived from `sample_scan.csv` — the synthetic
fixture — so even the population is invented.

**This is M14's problem, stated precisely:** pointing `_cash_pct` at `market_health_daily` needs
either a `pct_above_20dma` column added to the pipeline, or the desk's cash bands re-expressed
against the 50-day figure. The bands are calibrated on the 20-day number, so the second is a
strategy change and not a wiring change.

## Verdict

**The top-25 delta table is NOT empty. M13 does not open** (`MERGE-PROMPTS.md` rule 7).

It is much closer than the cell-level parity suggested — 23 of 25, with the top of the book stable
and scores agreeing to a tenth — and the gap now has a name. The next move is not a window
decision; it is **a corporate-action backfill**, which needs a source that serves history. That is
the same dependency `NEEDS-MAULIK.md` item 3 already carries for bar depth.
