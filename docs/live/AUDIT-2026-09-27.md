## 3. What `twt-auto` actually did — it tried, and Zerodha refused every order

The `twt-auto` container was recreated by Saturday's deploy (26 Sep 19:39), so its own logs hold no
weekday; its only line is `twt-auto: next run Mon 2026-09-28 09:15:10 IST`. The record is in the
plan lines it acted on (`tw_plan_line`, MORNING plans, last eight days):

```
2026-09-21  BUY_AT_OPEN x3  PROPOSED   (no session by 09:35 — login nudges fired 08:45 and 09:05)
2026-09-22  BUY_AT_OPEN x3  PROPOSED   (TW18 flipped the flag during the day; the 09:15 run had it off)
2026-09-23  BUY_AT_OPEN x3  REJECTED   "Market orders without market protection are not allowed via API. Please set market protect…"
2026-09-24  BUY_AT_OPEN x3  REJECTED   "Market orders without market protection are not allowed via API. Please set market protect…"
2026-09-25  BUY_AT_OPEN x1  PROPOSED   (login at 11:58 — the swing monitor's own start time that day; the plan had expired at 09:35)
```

**TWT AUTO sent six live MARKET buys on 23 and 24 Sep 2026 and the broker rejected all six** for
want of Kite's `market_protection` parameter, which `twt_execute._buy_at_open` never passed (the
swing book's buy does). `tw_session` has no row for those days because a rejection is not a confirm.
The sleeve's tested entry — the next open, at market — had therefore **never once reached the
exchange**, on a sleeve funded at ₹25,00,000 (`tw_config`) with auto-execute armed since 22 Sep.
Fixed in LV2 (DECISIONS-LV LV2.3): the buy carries `market_protection=-1`, Kite's own automatic
band. The first morning after that deploy with a Kite session before 09:15 is TWT's **first live
fill** — see `NEEDS-MAULIK.md` LV0.

The swing monitor ran every weekday (`sw_session.monitor_ran = t` for 18–25 Sep) and raised
2–6 signals a day, 0 fills; its start times record the login hour: 09:14 on 18, 23 and 24 Sep; 09:23
on 21 Sep; **10:40 on 22 Sep; 11:58 on 25 Sep**. Five names are `WATCHING`.

## 4. The token, and why "login is the event" matters

`/var/lib/baskfy/state/kite-token.enc` was rewritten at **00:04:15 IST on 28 Sep** — a login a few
minutes before this audit — and Kite tokens expire at 06:00, so that session is gone before the
open. The nudge files (`kite-login-nudge-<date>.first/.second`, written at 08:45 and 09:05 when no
session exists) exist for 7, 8, 10, 15, 16, 17, 21 and 23 Sep: on eight of the last fifteen trading
mornings there was no Kite session at 09:05. On those mornings `twt-auto` waited ten minutes and
gave up; the swing monitor started when the login came. That is gap 10 exactly, and LV4's brief.
